You MUST use the following syntax in your generated `description` to refer to call parameters.

*   **Variable Substitution:** Use `<%= variable_name %>` to insert a variable's value.

*   **Simplicity:** The template languages (Template-A for my instructions to you, and Template-B for your instructions to the agent) only support variable substitution and loops. Do not invent or use any other syntax.
